## Role

- Title: Data Analyst II, Member Insights team.
- Manager: Teresa Alvarez (Director, Member Insights).

## Main responsibilities

- Monthly KPI pack for branch managers: membership growth, product penetration, digital adoption, and attrition.
- Ad-hoc analysis requests from branches and marketing, usually two to five per week.
- Maintaining roughly 40 dbt models in the member_insights project, including tests and documentation.
- Tableau dashboards for the executive team (quarterly) and for branch managers (monthly).

## Recurring calendar

- Month-end close: the KPI pack is due on the 5th business day of each month. The three days before that are the busiest of the month; avoid scheduling coursework-heavy evenings then.
- Quarterly executive review: second week of January, April, July, and October.
- Team stand-up: Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 09:15.

## Constraints that affect my coursework

- Nothing from work systems may be copied into coursework or personal repositories.
- The capstone uses only synthetic data for this reason (see decision D-001).

## Skills I use at work that transfer to ML engineering

- Writing tested, documented SQL transformations with dbt.
- Explaining model-like outputs (risk scores, segments) to non-technical stakeholders.
- Working within data governance and privacy rules.

## Skills I do not get to practice at work
