## What the AI read from this

Before building anything, the AI summarized the threads it saw running through my work:

- **Weaving and grids:** my photo weavings on bark, the indigo loom pieces, the checkerboard floor in *Taking a Breather*, and the checkered bands in *Spare Sardines*.
- **Family memory painted like old film:** the wagon painting already uses my palette (mauve house, chartreuse lawn, periwinkle shirt, red wagon).
- **Fluid ink and gestural marks**, set against tight, controlled rendering.

From that it proposed *Woven Memory*: a digital version of my photo-weaving technique, weaving a family image toned like faded film with a fluid "pour" of color in my palette, with no landscapes.
